這場活動適合誰 Who Should Attend

無論你是 AI 新手，還是想提升工作效率的 職場專業人士，這場活動都會為你打開 Claude 協作的全新視野。我們會從最基礎的 Claude Desktop 操作開始，帶你一步步掌握 AI 協作技巧，再由業界重量級講者分享實戰案例。 Whether you're an AI beginner or a seasoned professional looking to boost productivity, this event will open up a whole new way of working with Claude. We'll start with the basics of Claude Desktop and guide you step by step into AI collaboration, followed by leading industry speakers sharing real-world use cases.
