主題演講 Featured Talk

「AI 許願學」 "AI Wish-Making Study"

講者：高見龍 Speaker: Kao Chien-Long

核心概念 Core Concept

Vibe Coding 就像跟 AI 許願——拿出一個精美的 prompt 當作祭品丟給 AI，然後它就把東西做出來了。但真的能成功嗎？

向 AI 許願得到的是流星還是隕石，取決於許願的方式。 Vibe Coding is like making a wish to AI—you offer a beautiful prompt as a sacrifice to the AI, and boom, it delivers. But does it really work that way?

Whether you get a shooting star or a meteorite from your AI wish depends on how you make the wish.

常見迷思 Common Misconceptions

  • Prompt 寫得不好？ Bad Prompt writing?
  • 模型不夠聰明？ Model not smart enough?
  • 訂閱費用不夠燒？ Not spending enough money on subscriptions?

真正的瓶頸是：你沒有把規格講清楚。 The real bottleneck is: you didn't spell out your specifications clearly.

這場分享你會學到 What You'll Learn

  • 為什麼你的 AI 願望常常變成隕石 Why your AI wishes often turn into meteorites
  • 如何將需求結構化為清晰的規格 How to structure requirements into clear specifications
  • 如何讓 AI 每次都從正確的起點開始 How to ensure the AI starts from the right place every time
  • 從「許願」到「下訂單」的轉變 From "making a wish" to "placing an order"
